AWS Marketplace Storefront

SaaSNova Storefront, our branded storefront on AWS Marketplace.

Storefront is SaaSNova's branded seller storefront on AWS Marketplace, featuring our Ignite, SuperNova, and NovaX programs alongside our PDMaaS, PMMaaS, PALaaS, PRMaaS, and PCMaaS services. It lets ISVs and enterprises find and procure SaaSNova's cloud GTM execution using their existing AWS buying relationship, reducing procurement friction and accelerating time-to-value.

About SaaSNova's Storefront

A familiar, auditable way to procure cloud GTM execution.

Storefront gives ISVs and enterprise buyers a simple, AWS-native way to discover and purchase SaaSNova's execution programs and managed services. Instead of a separate procurement process, buyers transact through AWS Marketplace, drawing down against their existing AWS commit and using the vendor onboarding, billing, and governance they already trust.

This marks a further step in SaaSNova's strategic relationship with AWS. As announced when SaaSNova was selected as a launch partner for AWS Marketplace Storefront, we're proud to bring our own branded storefront online, giving buyers a direct, board-ready path from "we need cloud GTM execution" to "it's procured and underway."

How Storefront Helps

Complements our sales motion, it doesn't replace it.

Storefront adds a route to market that simplifies purchasing for buyers and reduces complexity for internal teams. Every listed program follows AWS's Marketplace governance and co-sell frameworks.

Simple & Auditable

A familiar, auditable channel with clear visibility into every SaaSNova program and managed service available to purchase.

Faster Time-to-Value

Quicker access to pre-scoped, ready-to-start engagements, streamlining contract review and internal approvals.

Existing AWS Commit

Apply your existing AWS spend commitment toward SaaSNova engagements instead of competing for a separate budget line.
