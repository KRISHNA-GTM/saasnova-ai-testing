About SaaSNova Storefront

A familiar, auditable way to procure cloud GTM execution.

Storefront gives ISVs and enterprise buyers an AWS-native way to find and purchase SaaSNova's execution programs and managed services. Instead of running a separate procurement process, buyers transact through AWS Marketplace, draw down against their existing AWS commit, and use the vendor onboarding, billing and governance they already have in place.

This builds on SaaSNova's selection as a launch partner for AWS Marketplace Storefront. Every listed engagement is pre-scoped, so the path from "we need cloud GTM execution" to "it's procured and underway" is measured in days, not quarters.
