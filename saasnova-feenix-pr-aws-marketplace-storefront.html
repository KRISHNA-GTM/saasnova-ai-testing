SaaSNova Selected as Launch Partner for AWS Marketplace Storefront

June 16, 2026 New York, NY

NEW YORK, NY – June 16, 2026 – SaaSNova, a women‑owned cloud go‑to‑market (GTM) acceleration firm, today announced its selection as a launch partner for the new AWS Marketplace Storefront, a feature designed to help AWS ISVs and channel partners expand their cloud revenue through simplified, branded marketplace experiences.

As a launch partner, SaaSNova will support AWS partners in the development and execution of their GTM co-sell campaigns, expanding their global marketplace business through their branded Storefront. Partners can showcase their solutions in private offer campaigns, streamline marketplace transactions, and scale co‑sell engagement with AWS field teams directly from their Storefront.

Partner Perspectives

"SaaSNova is proud to support the launch of AWS Marketplace Storefront and help partners unlock faster, more predictable cloud revenue. By simplifying marketplace operations and reducing procurement friction, we enable ISVs and channel partners to focus on what matters most: executing high‑velocity GTM motions and building repeatable revenue engines with AWS."
Jen Dawson
Founder & CEO, SaaSNova

Customers benefit from a trusted, familiar purchasing experience powered by AWS Marketplace infrastructure. Organizations can leverage their existing AWS accounts to streamline procurement, consolidate billing, and accelerate deployment timelines.

SaaSNova’s role in the launch includes helping partners build and execute cloud GTM strategies, accelerate marketplace adoption, and expand co‑sell velocity. In addition, SaaSNova is a participant in various AWS incentive programs, further assisting partners with their AWS Marketplace operations to accelerate their Storefront launch and overall cloud revenue growth.
