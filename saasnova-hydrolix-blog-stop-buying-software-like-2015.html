Skip the procurement wait and buy Hydrolix on the AWS Marketplace through your existing AWS account and committed spend.

You’ve already decided you want Hydrolix. Then you wait.

Security reviews and procurement can take months or even quarters, but you can make the process much faster when you’re buying through your existing Amazon account.

That’s the gap we set out to close. Not the technology, the paperwork.

Now there’s a Hydrolix storefront on AWS Marketplace. This branded buying experience is built straight into the marketplace you already use, making it easy to get started.

Buy in Days, Not Quarters

Discover, evaluate, and buy Hydrolix without ever leaving AWS:

  • Choose offerings built for Media and Entertainment or Security, and find your fit fast.
  • Get custom pricing through a private offer, then buy with your existing AWS account. The charge lands on the bill already in place and draws down your committed spend. No new budget line. No new Master Services Agreement (MSA).
  • Trade a months-long procurement exercise for a purchase measured in days, because the deal runs on the account and the commitment you already have.

Ready When You Are

In 2015, buying enterprise software meant a stack of procurement approvals, months of security review, a fresh contract to redline, and a purchase order inching through finance. You waited a quarter to start using something you’d already decided to buy. That was the cost of doing business. It isn’t anymore.

The platform you’ve already scoped. The account you already run. The commitment you already made. Now they meet in one place, and buying Hydrolix takes days, not quarters. So you get to what matters sooner: keeping all of your full-fidelity, AI-ready data, without the cost tradeoffs that force other teams to delete what they’ll need later.
