What FQ Source is

A joint initiative to make women-owned technology companies enterprise-ready.

FQ Source is a collaboration between AWS, SaaSNova and The Female Quotient. It builds the capabilities, visibility and enterprise readiness women-owned technology companies need to compete for procurement opportunities and grow through AWS Marketplace.

Marketplace readiness

Get your products and services listed, priced and published on AWS Marketplace.

Go-to-market execution

Build the campaign, channel and co-sell motions that turn AWS presence into pipeline.

Procurement pathways

Reach enterprise and government buyers through the channels they already purchase in.

FQ Source builds on the Women-Owned Cloud Innovation Storefront, which already features 273 women-owned businesses on AWS Marketplace.

Who it's for

Built for women-owned and women-led technology companies.

You do not need AWS Marketplace experience to join. Most participants start with no listing and no AWS Partner registration.

  • Women-owned or women-led technology companies
  • SaaS, cloud, consulting and innovation suppliers
  • Companies seeking procurement access and enterprise buyers
  • Companies ready to scale through AWS Marketplace
