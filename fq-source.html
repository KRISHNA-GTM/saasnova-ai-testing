What FQ Source is

A joint initiative by AWS, The Female Quotient and SaaSNova.

FQ Source gets women-owned technology companies ready to sell to enterprise and government buyers, and puts them in front of those buyers through AWS Marketplace. SaaSNova does the execution with you. You are not handed a playbook and left to run it.

01 / Readiness

Get listed and get found

Your product goes live on AWS Marketplace, packaged and priced, with your own branded storefront page.

02 / Demand

Turn presence into pipeline

Campaigns, partner motions and co-selling with AWS sellers, so the listing produces deals instead of sitting there.

03 / Procurement

Sell the way buyers buy

Enterprise and government buyers purchase through AWS Marketplace using budget already committed to AWS. We set you up to sell into it.

Who it's for

Women-owned and women-led technology companies.

What you get

Four things you walk away owning.

SaaSNova builds and deploys all four with you, as the execution partner between AWS and participating companies.

01

Your Marketplace listing

We package your product, set your pricing, prepare the terms buyers have to agree to, and take the listing through AWS review until it is live and buyable.

You walk away withA live AWS Marketplace listing
02

Your own storefront

A branded page on AWS Marketplace where buyers see everything you sell in one place. You are also placed in the Women-Owned Cloud Innovation Storefront alongside the companies already there.

You walk away withA deployed, branded storefront
03

Co-selling with AWS

Co-selling means AWS sellers can bring your product into their own customer conversations. We register your deals in ACE, the system those sellers work from, and build the campaigns behind it.

You walk away withDeals registered with AWS sellers
04

A route into procurement

A private offer lets you sell to one buyer at a price and terms you negotiate. Because it runs through AWS Marketplace, the buyer can pay using budget already committed to AWS.

You walk away withPrivate offers ready to send
